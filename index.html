A Palace of Timeless Flavors & Ancestral Mastery

Founded on the revered principles of royal court kitchens, Deluxe Mahal restores the ceremonial art of Awadhi and Mughlai hospitality. Our Khansamas—lineage chefs whose ancestors catered to Nizams and Nawabs—still measure wild mountain cardamom, saffron filaments, and edible silver leaves by eye and instinct.

Every dish undergoes painstaking slow-cooking within hand-sealed clay handis, coaxing aromatics into rich, velour sauces without rush or compromise. Here, dining ceases to be a mere meal; it emerges as an aristocratic ritual.
