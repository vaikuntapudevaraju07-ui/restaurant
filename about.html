Deluxe Mahal brings the warmth of royal Mughlai and Awadhi hospitality to the modern dining table, with recipes made slowly and welcomes given generously.

Our history

Recipes worth keeping, hospitality worth sharing.

Founded in 1999, Deluxe Mahal began with a simple ambition: preserve the patient craft and generous spirit of the old royal kitchens. Our cooks draw on Mughlai and Awadhi traditions, layering whole spices, tending slow-cooked gravies, and sealing biryani over gentle heat.

Today, guests gather here for family meals, celebrations, and quiet evenings alike. The setting has evolved; our care for the food and the people around the table has not.

Our vision
