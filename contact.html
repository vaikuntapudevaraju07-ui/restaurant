Dining Etiquette & Inquiries

Refined details to assist in orchestrating your visit to Deluxe Mahal.

We uphold an elegant **Smart Casual to Royal Formal** aesthetic. Traditional ethnic finery, dinner jackets, tailored trousers, and collared shirts are warmly welcomed. We kindly request guests to refrain from beachwear, athletic shorts, flip-flops, or baseball caps in both the Main Dining Hall and the Sheesh Mahal salon.
Yes, complimentary white-glove valet services are provided at the North Gate Portico for all dining and banquet guests. For patrons arriving with private chauffeurs, a sheltered lounge with refreshments is available adjacent to the carriage turnaround.
Private Salon reservations (Sheesh Mahal and Darbar Pavilions for 9 to 60 guests) are curated with custom multi-course degustation menus starting at $95 per guest. Our private banquet team will prepare personalized silver-foil printed menus, dedicated musicians, and bespoke floral arrangements upon consultation.
Tables for up to 8 guests may be rescheduled or cancelled without fee up to 6 hours prior to the reservation time via your confirmation link or direct concierge call. For larger festive groups and salon bookings, we request at least 24 hours advance notice to allow our master khansamas to adjust culinary prep.
